# Klar — 3-Minute Pitch Script

> Total time: ~3 minutes. ~25 seconds per slide.
> Delivery: conversational, confident, specific. No filler. Every sentence earns its place.

---

## [TITLE SLIDE] — 5 seconds

**"Hi, we're Klar."**

---

## [SLIDE 1] Problem + Customer — 30 seconds

Imagine you just moved to Germany for your master's degree. You open your mailbox and there's a letter from the Auslanderbehorde — the immigration office. It's in legal German. You can't read it. But buried inside is a 14-day deadline, and if you miss it, you lose your right to stay in the country.

This happens to 402,000 international students and over 113,000 Blue Card holders in Germany — every single year. Right now, their options are: pay a lawyer 150 euros for 20 minutes, panic-post on Reddit at midnight, or ask a German friend and hope they're right.

Berlin alone has a 3-to-6 month wait for a residence permit appointment. One missed deadline, one wrong form — and you're starting over.

---

## [SLIDE 2] Solution + Product — 30 seconds

Klar fixes this in 27 seconds. You upload your letter — photo, scan, or PDF. Klar does four things no competitor does together.

First, it reads and explains the letter in your language — English, Turkish, Arabic, whatever you speak. Second, it flags the deadline and scores the risk: "miss this and your permit is rejected." Third, it drafts the response letter for you, in proper Behordendeutsch, with a document checklist. Fourth, if there's a form, Klar detects every field, asks you simple questions in your language, and returns the form filled out — ready to print and submit.

Every legal citation comes from our RAG pipeline over 13 German laws and 1,488 actual paragraphs. No hallucinated law. Real citations.

---

## [SLIDE 3] Why Now — 25 seconds

Three things changed. First, vision language models now hit 96% accuracy on document understanding — reading faded stamps, handwritten notes, dense legal German. This was impossible two years ago.

Second, Germany's digitization has stalled. The government promised 6,000 services online by 2022. They delivered 115. They dropped the deadline entirely. This gap isn't closing — it's our market.

Third, the customer base is surging. The Skilled Immigration Act, the new Chancenkarte visa, record Blue Card issuance — Germany issued 56,000 Blue Cards in 2024 alone, 72% of the entire EU total. More people are coming, and they all hit the same wall.

---

## [SLIDE 4] Market + Competition — 30 seconds

Let me size this bottom-up. Our beachhead is 700,000 high-need users: international students, Blue Card holders, Chancenkarte arrivals. At 7.99 a month, that's a 67 million euro SAM. We're targeting 22,000 paying users in three years — just over 2 million in ARR. Conservative. Defensible.

Now, competition. We have competitors — but look at this matrix. Admina decodes letters but doesn't draft replies, fill forms, or cite law. Papeer drafts replies but doesn't fill forms or cite law. SmartBurokratie cites law but doesn't decode letters or draft replies. No one does all four. And critically — not a single direct competitor has raised institutional VC funding. The quadrant we occupy is empty.

---

## [SLIDE 5] Business Model + Evidence — 25 seconds

Our pricing is simple. First letter free — show the value instantly. Then 7.99 a month for unlimited letters, form fills, and follow-up questions.

Is that defensible? These users already pay: 12,000 euros a year in a blocked account, 90 to 160 euros for Fintiba setup, 95 to 150 a month for health insurance, and 150-plus euros every time they see a lawyer. Eight euros a month is nothing against the cost of a missed deadline.

We target freemium conversion at 3 to 5%, which is the benchmark for good freemium products. Unit economics: LTV around 50 to 96 euros, CAC under 20 euros through organic community channels. That's a healthy 3-to-1 or better.

---

## [SLIDE 6] Go-to-Market — 20 seconds

We start where we are: TUM and LMU in Munich, 15,000 international students. We are the customer — we're in the WhatsApp groups, at orientation week, in the ESN chapters. That's our first 50 users in month one.

From there, we expand through expat communities — India is the number one source country with 59,000 students — Facebook groups, Reddit, Telegram. Zero-CAC organic channels.

Parallel to that: B2B2C. University international offices and employer relocation packages. One signed university pilot is the most fundable proof point at this stage, and Ridocu's pivot to B2B independently validates this demand.

---

## [SLIDE 7] Team — 15 seconds

Why us? Because we lived it. We are international students in Germany. We've paid the 150-euro lawyer. We've panicked over the Auslanderbehorde letter. We've stared at German forms for hours. We built Klar in six hours at a hackathon because this problem is personal. We have a working pipeline — OCR, agent, RAG, response generation, form fill — all running end to end.

We're Klar. We'd love to talk.

## Delivery Notes

- **Open with the story**, not stats. "Imagine you just moved to Germany..." pulls the audience in.
- **Slide 2 is your demo moment.** If you have a live demo or video, play it here. The pipeline visual on the slide is a fallback.
- **Slide 4 is where you win the room.** The competitive matrix is your strongest visual — point to the empty row at the bottom and say "no one does all four."
- **Don't rush Slide 5.** The price anchoring (€150 lawyer vs. €8/month) is the single most memorable number. Pause and let it land.
- **End fast.** Slide 7 should feel like a mic drop, not a summary. "We're Klar. We'd love to talk." — then stop.
- **Replace [Name] placeholders** on the team slide with actual names and photos before presenting.
- **Replace contact info** (team@klar.app, klar.app, demo.klar.app) with real URLs.
